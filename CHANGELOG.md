# Changelog

## 0.9.0

The first release as **Chiaki-webOS** (previously chiaki-lg).

- New name, new icon and a new app ID. Open the new app once and your consoles and settings from chiaki-lg are copied over; then remove the old app.
- Add consoles right on the TV: sign in to PSN with your phone, enter the console's PIN, done.
- Each console can use its own PSN account, and accounts you have signed in with are offered again when you add the next console.
- Typing uses the TV's own keyboard.
- The Magic Remote pointer works everywhere and hides when you use the arrow keys, like in LG's own apps.
- Back on the remote goes one step back on every screen, and the start screen asks before closing the app.
- During a game, Back or a single Down asks "Leave the game?" (with "Leave + rest mode" if rest mode is set to Ask), instead of Down twice.
- Fixed: the app did not start on webOS 4 TVs (a missing library), and showing the connection info could close it there.
- Fixed: waking the console over the home network did not work.
- Fixed: settings could not be saved right after installing until the TV was restarted.
